How we calculate this Methodology behind every number in this tool
Three cost concepts — kept strictly separate
Actual costReal notification volume × local SMS rate, taken directly from Clickatell/Twilio billing files. What was actually spent.
Cost at full scaleWhat a journey would cost in a country if it sent messages at the same rate the US does per order — used because no other market has its own behavioral data yet.
Cost at an opt-in scenarioA separate dial: what cost looks like if the share of customers opted in to SMS changes, independent of how often an opted-in customer gets messaged.
The SMS Cost Calculator (Tabs 4 & 5)

Projected cost = selected country's order volume × US participation rate for that event × local SMS cost × billing units per message.

Participation rate is measured from real US 2025 order and notification volumes (Power BI) — the share of orders that actually triggered each SMS event. Every other country borrows this rate as a proxy, since the US is the only market with its own behavioral history on file.

The SMS opt-in scenario slider is a separate multiplier on top of this: baseline is today's real US opt-in rate (2,181,880 of 8,375,496 US customers, ≈26%). Moving the slider scales projected volume by the ratio of target opt-in to that baseline — assuming newly opted-in customers behave like already-opted-in ones on average.

Global Deployment Cost (Tabs 6 & 7)

Same participation-rate proxy model, applied across all markets with order-volume data on file — 139 markets on Clickatell rates, 46 on Twilio (the subset of the 57 Twilio-contracted countries that also have order volume on record). Countries without order data are listed rather than estimated, so nothing here is a guess dressed up as a number.

What "Live" means here

Message Flow Map, Messages Library, and Customer Journeys only show messages marked Live = Yes in the source Google Sheet. That filter now happens server-side — the dashboard requests only live rows from the sheet, rather than downloading everything and hiding the rest client-side.

Customer Journeys uses a separate hardcoded dataset (not sheet-fed), so its messages are cross-matched against the live sheet by title to decide what to show.

Data sources
  • Order volumes — Power BI, all B&M markets, 2025
  • Message templates — Google Sheet mirroring the Ria transactional message library (B&M + Digital tabs)
  • SMS costs (Clickatell) — current contracted telco rates
  • SMS costs (Twilio) — Order Form 00142240.0, Exhibit A rate schedule, effective Aug 1, 2025
  • Billing units — most Ria SMS messages run ~200 characters, spanning 2 telco billing units of 160 characters each; editable per calculation.
